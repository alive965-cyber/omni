# Section 1: High-Frequency Compound FACS Combinations

## Triad 1: `AU01 + AU02 + AU04` (Brow Raise + Brow Furrow)
- **Visual Appearance:** Eyebrows pulled together and raised centrally, creating horizontal forehead wrinkles and vertical glabellar wrinkles simultaneously.
- **Behavioral Meaning:** **Apprehensive Concern, Fearful Anticipation, or Incredulous Perplexity.** Distinct from pure surprise (`AU01+AU02+AU25` with relaxed `AU04`) and pure concentration (`AU04` alone).

## Dyad 2: `AU04 + AU24` (Brow Lowerer + Lip Presser)
- **Visual Appearance:** Lowered, knitted brows paired with tightly compressed lips.
- **Behavioral Meaning:** **Controlled Frustration, Determination, or Active Disagreement While Listening.** When occurring while the interlocutor is speaking, the subject is silently formulating an objection or suppressing an interruption.

## Triad 3: `AU01 + AU04 + AU15` (Oblique Brows + Lip Corner Depressor)
- **Visual Appearance:** Inner corners of eyebrows raised and drawn together into an inverted-V shape, while mouth corners pull downward.
- **Behavioral Meaning:** **Resignation, Sadness, Disappointment, or Empathy.** Among the hardest facial configurations to produce voluntarily without genuine negative-valence affect.

## Dyad 4: `AU12 (Asymmetric) + AU04` or Unilateral `AU12` (Contempt / Skeptical Smirk)
- **Visual Appearance:** One lip corner pulled up/tightened (`facial_asymmetry_index > 0.30`) with absent `AU06` and slight brow lowering or head tilt back.
- **Behavioral Meaning:** **Skeptical Dismissal, Irony, Sarcasm, or Superiority Appraisal.** When paired with positive spoken words (*"Sure, that's a great idea"*), it is a primary visual marker of verbal-visual sarcasm.

## Triad 5: `AU12 + AU15 + AU24` (The Ambivalent / Suppressed Smile-Frown)
- **Visual Appearance:** Zygomaticus upward pull (`AU12`) competing simultaneously with depressor anguli oris (`AU15`) and lip compression (`AU24`).
- **Behavioral Meaning:** **Affective Ambivalence or Politeness Masking Over Disappointment.** The speaker is attempting to maintain a pleasant social facade while experiencing negative evaluation.
